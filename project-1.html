Overview

Parada Façade is a French exterior renovation company that needed a website to present its façade services clearly and build trust with homeowners and property managers. I designed and built a responsive, service-led website where visitors can quickly understand what the company offers and get in touch.

The challenge

Renovation work is a high-trust purchase. The site had to look professional, explain several related services without overwhelming visitors, and make the next step — requesting a quote — obvious on every screen size.

My approach

  • Structured the site around the company's core services so each one has a clear, scannable section.
  • Built every layout mobile-first in Elementor, then scaled up for tablet and desktop.
  • Placed contact and quote calls-to-action at natural decision points across the page.
  • Optimised images and scripts to keep pages light and fast.
